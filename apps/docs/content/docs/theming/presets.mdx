---
title: Presets
description: Flat, glass, neumorphic and skeuomorphic — when to use each, and what to watch out for.
---

A preset is one stylesheet that redefines the whole token contract under a
`data-style` attribute. `<UIProvider>` puts that attribute on `<html>` and
keeps it in sync with the user's choice.

---

## Flat

Clean and minimal. Structure comes from borders, shadows are barely there,
and nothing distracts from content. This is the default preset — the right
choice for dense, information-first UIs, admin panels, and anywhere you
want your own brand color to do the talking.

<PresetGrid only="flat" />

**Caution:** flat surfaces live and die by contrast. If you override
`--background`, keep `--foreground` at WCAG AA (4.5:1) — and remember
`--card` defaults to the same value as `--background`, so separation comes
from `--border` and `--surface-shadow`, not color shifts.

Activated by `data-style="flat"`.

## Glass

Translucent surfaces floating over a blurred, softly colorful backdrop.
Depth comes from what you can see *through* each layer. Great for
marketing pages, media overlays, and apps that want a premium, spatial
feel.

<PresetGrid only="glass" />

**Caution (known gotcha): glass needs contrast behind it.** It reads
badly over a flat single-color background, so this preset paints a fixed
gradient on `<body>` — in your own layouts, make sure there is imagery or
gradient behind glass surfaces. Also **cap nested glass surfaces around
2**: `backdrop-filter` is not free, and stacking blurs kills scroll
performance on low-end devices.

Activated by `data-style="glass"`.

## Neumorphic

Soft, extruded shapes on a single base tone — dual light shadows make
elements look punched out of the page itself. Distinctive and tactile;
best for focused, low-density interfaces (players, dashboards with few
big controls, smart-home style UIs).

<PresetGrid only="neumorphic" />

**Caution (known gotcha): neumorphism's `--card` must equal
`--background`.** Any drift breaks the "extruded from the page" illusion —
if you override either, override both to the same value. Contrast is the
other risk: keep an eye on `--muted-foreground` on the shared background,
and check that focus rings (via `--ring`) are legible on the gray field.

Activated by `data-style="neumorphic"`.

## Skeuomorphic

Real-world materials: paper background, glossy vertical gradients, beveled
edges and honest borders. Nostalgic and fun; use it for playful products,
game-adjacent UIs, or whenever you want controls that look pressable.

<PresetGrid only="skeuomorphic" />

**Caution:** the gloss comes from `--surface-image` layered over each
surface's background color. If you override `--card`, pick a value light
(or dark) enough that the white gloss gradient stays visible — very
saturated base colors swallow it.

Activated by `data-style="skeuomorphic"`.
